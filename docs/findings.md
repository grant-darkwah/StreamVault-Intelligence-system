## Analytical methodology and limitations

- **Franchise classification:** TMDb collection membership is used as a proxy. Collections with at least two sampled titles are ranked as multi-film collections.
- **Standalone classification:** A standalone candidate is a film with no TMDb collection association. A title in a single-film collection is not automatically classified as standalone.
- **Composite ranking:** Equal-weighted percentile ranks are calculated for vote average, vote count, popularity and reported revenue. The resulting score supports candidate prioritization, not financial valuation.
- **Financial comparison:** Revenue-to-budget ratio is descriptive only. It does not account for marketing, distribution, licensing, or other costs. Historical figures are not normalized for inflation or currency differences.
- **Talent analysis:** Director and lead-cast recurrence is descriptive. It does not establish causation or guarantee future commercial performance.
- **Acquisition diligence:** Actual bundle rights, territory, exclusivity, licensing cost, streaming outcomes and contract terms remain unverified.
